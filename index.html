Execution Pipeline

From raw remittance denial to citation-backed internal appeal packet.

STEP 01 Queue Intake

Denial Matching

Ingests 835/ERA remittance streams and billing workqueues to map CARC/RARC reason codes, CPT/HCPCS modifiers, payer rules, and timely-filing deadlines.

→ Classified Denial Profile
STEP 02 Coverage Logic

Policy Extraction

Cross-references denied service lines against active CMS LCD/NCD guidelines, NCCI edits, and commercial payer medical policies to pull exact reconsideration criteria.

→ Verified Policy Citations
STEP 03 Clinical Context

Chart Assembly

Retrieves and indexes relevant encounter documentation, operative notes, diagnostic findings, and prior-authorization records mapped directly to each cited policy rule.

→ Mapped Chart Excerpts
STEP 04 Packet Output

Appeal Generation

Assembles matched codes, policy citations, and supporting clinical chart notes into a structured internal appeal packet ready for biller review and submission.

→ Submission-Ready Packet

Operational Focus

Billing companies process claim denials daily through internal payer appeal channels. Payer Delta automates this repetitive paperwork—ensuring every appeal includes the exact codes, clinical chart notes, and policy citations required for swift reconsideration.

Instead of forcing billing specialists to hunt across payer portals, PDF policy manuals, and EHR attachments for every denied line item, Payer Delta prepares the complete appeal record upfront so your team can review, approve, and submit at volume.
