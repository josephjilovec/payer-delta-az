Medical billing teams work across remittance queues, source documents, and supporting records. The proposed Payer Delta workflow brings that preparation into a structured file so the next reviewer can see the basis and the gaps.

Automation is useful when its inputs and boundaries can be inspected. The example shows the intended preparation sequence; it does not demonstrate a live EHR connection, verified policy lookup, or submitted appeal.
