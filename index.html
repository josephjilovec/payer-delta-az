+ BEFORE WE TALK

Questions
before you book.

Does Payer Delta replace our billing team?

No. The recovery, RCM support, intelligence, and forecasting scopes are designed to work with your practice or billing team. Your agreement defines the work each party owns.

Who signs and submits a correction or appeal?

Your authorized practice team reviews the prepared materials and owns signing and submission. Missing sources, supporting records, or authority need to be resolved before that handoff is ready.

What should I send in the first inquiry?

Start with your organization, your role, and the workflow you want to discuss on the introductory call. Please do not send patient records, remittances, or claim exports through the booking form. Data-handling requirements, any necessary agreements, and a suitable transfer process must be confirmed before records are requested.

Is there one appeal deadline for every claim?

No. Filing requirements depend on the payer, plan or product, type of action, review stage, and applicable dates. For Original Medicare fee-for-service, a first-level redetermination generally must be requested within 120 days after receipt of the initial claim determination. Commercial plan requirements must be checked for the specific claim. Review the applicable notice before the payer’s appeal deadline. CMS first-level appeal guidance ↗

Does the 22% fee apply to every service?

The 22% fee applies to commercial denial recovery. Medicare packet preparation and optional ongoing RCM, payer intelligence, or forecasting services are priced separately and agreed before work begins.
