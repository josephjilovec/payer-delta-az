2. Administrative & Analytical Scope

Administrative Boundary Notice

Payer Delta Arizona is an independent revenue cycle management (RCM) analytics, denial redetermination research, and appeal packet preparation service. Payer Delta is NOT a law firm, does NOT provide legal advice or formal administrative legal representation, and does NOT practice medicine or render clinical determinations.

Our services encompass: (a) parsing electronic remittance advice (ANSI X12 835) and CARC/RARC denial reason codes; (b) researching commercial payer clinical policy bulletins, Local Coverage Determinations (LCDs), and billing manuals; (c) drafting structured appeal evidence packets and redetermination letters; and (d) providing predictive cash forecasting and revenue integrity surveillance.

Mandatory Provider Signing & Submission Firewall

Payer Delta does not directly sign provider appeal declarations or submit claims under its own entity name. The client practice represents and warrants that all documentation submitted to payers has been reviewed, approved, and authorized by qualified clinical or billing personnel.

4. Commercial Denial Recovery Fees (22% Contingency)

Commercial denial recovery services are provided strictly under the following performance terms:

  • 22% Contingency Fee: Client agrees to pay Payer Delta twenty-two percent (22%) of the gross collected cash recovered from commercial insurance payers on accounts, claims, or line items worked, prepared, or appealed by Payer Delta.
  • Direct Payer Disbursements: Payer Delta never takes possession of client collections. All insurance disbursements are paid by the payer directly to the provider's practice account.
  • Performance Firewall: If no commercial reimbursement is recovered and received by the practice on a worked claim, zero commercial recovery fee is owed.
  • Invoicing & Payment: Invoices are issued following verified provider receipt of funds (demonstrated via 835 remittance or payment voucher) and are payable within fifteen (15) calendar days of invoice date.
