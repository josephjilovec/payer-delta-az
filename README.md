# Payer Delta

**Live Site:** [https://payerdelta.josephjilovec.com](https://payerdelta.josephjilovec.com)  
**Sector:** Healthcare RCM & Operational Automation

Automated denial management and internal appeal execution for medical billing teams.

## Summary

An automated workflow engine designed to eliminate denial backlogs. Payer Delta integrates directly with billing pipelines to match denial codes, extract policy citations, assemble chart notes, and generate structured internal appeal packets.

## Execution Pipeline

1. **Denial Matching** — Ingests remittance feeds and billing queues to classify CARC/RARC denial codes, modifiers, and filing windows.
2. **Policy Extraction** — Extracts governing coverage rules and policy citations from CMS LCD/NCD guidelines and commercial payer policies.
3. **Chart Assembly** — Indexes and aligns clinical encounter documentation and diagnostic findings to the cited payer criteria.
4. **Appeal Generation** — Compiles structured, citation-backed internal appeal packets ready for billing-team review and submission.
